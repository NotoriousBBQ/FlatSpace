- **AI avoids blockaded routes and uses blockades**
  - (1) Colonization done: blockaded planets build no colony ships and do not colonize; colonization routes avoid visible blockaded planets and cancel when none exists; blockaded planets get a tunable warship/upgrade boost (`blockadedWarshipBoost`); see CLAUDE.md "Warships and Blockade". *(moved 2026-10-01)*
  - (2) Resource shipping — done: shipments route around visible blockaded planets (the route may exceed the maximum range); with no clean route they ship only if the amount exceeds the total blockade values along the least-loss route, otherwise they are cancelled. A blockaded source or target is an ordinary node on the route (its value counts), and a shipment is now cut at a blockaded origin on its first turn; see CLAUDE.md "Warships and Blockade". *(moved 2026-10-01)*
  - (3) Assault breaks blockades done: under Consolidate the assault targets planets visibly blockaded against the player first (ranked by offense already committed, a cut of the player's own orders within `blockadeTargetRecentTurns`, the smallest offense still needed, path cost and name), sizes the force by real warship offense (value x (1 + `blockadeBreakMargin`) minus offense in flight, ships taken from the cheapest sources), holds a broken blockade so the force does not leave and let it re-form (`AssaultPlanner.ContestedHolds`; a held colony is sink-only), and boosts Warship Offense research while blockaded (`blockadedOffenseResearchBoost`). Logged as `BlockadeTarget`, `BlockadeForce`, `BlockadeTargetEnd`, `OffenseResearchBoost` and `BlockadeSkipped`; see CLAUDE.md "Warships and Blockade" and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-01-blockade-breaking-design.md`. A large committed force outranking a cheap blockade is the desired outcome, not a defect. *(moved 2026-10-01)*
  - (4) Connectivity for garrison and colonization targeting done: `PlanetCentrality` scores every planet by its shortest-path betweenness percentile from the stored all-pairs paths (built once at board init, not saved); garrison category 4 is now "chokepoint" (percentile >= `chokepointPercentile`, default 0.9) and under Consolidate a colonized chokepoint keeps a garrison (round 1) beside outer planets; the blockade-breaking target ranking gains a chokepoint step after the recent-cut step (only real chokepoints count, so a `chokepointPercentile` above 1 switches it off); Consolidate colonization tilts toward chokepoints (`colonizationChokepointWeight`, default 0.5; Expand unchanged; the order delay uses the real route cost). Logged as `Chokepoints`, `ChokepointColonize` and `ChokepointGarrison`; chokepoint garrisons also raise the wanted fleet (`WantedWarships`). See CLAUDE.md "Connectivity (chokepoints)" and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-02-connectivity-targeting-design.md`. *(moved 2026-10-02)*
  - (5) Colonization avoidance for orders already in flight done: each turn after `ApplyBlockades`, `ColonistRedirect` re-plans a colonist whose remaining route crosses a planet in its owner's `BlockadeView` (visible planets plus blockade memory): a clean detour to the same target, else a diversion to another candidate (known planets that are empty, have the player's colonist already inbound, or are colonized below max population, own planets included; ordinary launches keep their rules), with the delay restarted from the new route's real cost and the food rider and transfer flags following; a detour costing more than `colonistDetourDivertRatio` (default 2) times the best diversion is declined for it. A colonist arriving at a full planet it owns docks as a colony ship (a full planet of another player still takes it), and a blockade cut removes only its own colonist and rider. `GameAIMap.OrderPathPoints` gives display code (fog corridors, order lines) the carried route. See CLAUDE.md "Warships and Blockade". Tuning on `4p.json` (8 runs against 6): colonist cuts per run 9-35 to 4-11, colonize arrive/start 0.76-0.92 to 0.92-0.98; with the ratio rule detours fell from 14 averaging cost 1787 (79% never arrived) to 3 averaging 853. *(moved 2026-10-02)*
- **Milestone: ship_combat** — warships stop being only a blockade and garrison force and can fight, which needs players to have a relationship first (the owner's term for a grouping of features forming one major completion milestone).
  - (1) Simplified diplomacy simulation done: every ordered pair of players holds a Peace or War stance (`DiplomacyState` on `GameAIMap`), driven by a per-pair hostility score that each turn decays 5% and gains the rival's blockade cuts on my orders (x5), its warships docked on my planets or beside them (x0.5, not its garrison on a planet it populates), and 1 x log2(my fleet strength / its visible fleet strength) clamped to +-2 (`FleetStrength` = sum of Offense x (Health + Defense)). `StanceMatrix` decides War or Peace per rival (a `ScoreMatrix` with the new `IndependentRows` flag; a logistic of the score with midpoint 30 and steepness 4, the held stance x3, a 10-turn hold); war needs only one side and the other side is forced into it once it has contact with the declarer. War puts a Consolidate player into Amass and peace with every rival returns it to Consolidate; Amass runs the Expand routine with its own research and industry tables (Warship 4.0, ColonyShip 1.0 on industry) and inherits every "Consolidate only" rule through `PlayerAI.IsConsolidateLike`. The assault attacks only players at war (the force is sized from their fleets only, `WantedWarships` adds the assault force only against a war rival, and `ContestedHolds` releases a standoff on a peaceful rival's planet unless it is blockaded against me); a war without contact decays and can end. Stances persist in saves (`PlayerSave.stances`); `diplomacyEnabled` false (and any self-check map) is the legacy mode with every rival an enemy. Logged as `Stance`, `WarForced` and `Hostility`. See CLAUDE.md "Diplomacy" and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-diplomacy-design.md`. Tuning (steepness 8 to 4, then Amass ColonyShip 0.5 to 1.0): on `test2.json` wars fell from 29 to 16 per run and random early wars all but vanished; on `4p.json` the 0.5 weight cost about 10 planets and 23% of colonization arrivals by T375 and 1.0 restored them to the no-diplomacy range (planets 96 against 98, arrivals 149 against 153, research 94 against 97); fleet-cap violations stayed 0 in every run. *(moved 2026-10-04)*
  - Prerequisite for (2): diplomacy as orders and simultaneous player decisions done: stance changes are immediate orders (`OrderTypeDeclareWar`, `OrderTypeMakePeace`, never in `CurrentAIOrders`) emitted by `PlayerAI.UpdateDiplomacy(turn, orders)` and applied in `ProcessNewOrders` through the pure `GameAI.ApplyStanceOrder`, so every player decides against the same stances; the Consolidate/Amass switch and the `WarForced` lines moved to `PlayerAI.ApplyWarState` at the start of `ProcessResults` (a rival's declaration is first seen one turn later, a declarer's own Amass switch one turn later). An audit of `ProcessResults` found no other cross-player write, and `SimultaneitySelfCheck` runs three players forward and reversed (re-seeding `GameAI.Rand` per player) and asserts the same orders, stances and strategy per player. See CLAUDE.md "Diplomacy" (Simultaneity) and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-diplomacy-orders-simultaneity-design.md`. Tuning on `test2.json` (6 runs against 6): every metric overlaps the earlier runs; the one intended change is that a declaration is seen 1 turn later in 47 of 47 cases (it was 0 or 1 depending on player id). *(moved 2026-10-05)*
  - (2) Ship to ship combat done: docked warships of players at war that share a planet fight once a turn (`CombatSystem.Resolve`, pure, run after arrivals and before the planet update, off in legacy mode): a start-of-turn snapshot, each side's effective offense split among its war rivals by hostility toward each (floor 1), one pool per victim, focus fire on the lowest-health-fraction ship scaled by `K / (K + Defense)` (`combatDamageK` 20), applied simultaneously, losses attributed per attacker (`PlanetUpdateResultTypeWarshipsLost`). Damage is persistent (`Ship.Damage`, stored so older saves load at full health), travels with a fleet in flight and in saves (`ShipFleetPayload.Damage`, `ShipSave.damage`), and a damaged ship deals proportionally less (`WarshipStats.EffectiveOffense`, no floor), which every offense reader uses (strength, blockade, assault, in-flight offense, fleet row); warships heal 10% a turn at a planet their owner populates with no at-war warship present, and a player's colony ships die when it has no warship at a planet and an at-war rival has one (everywhere). Hostility gains 1 per ship lost and loses 4 a turn while the share of fleet strength lost over 10 turns is at least 0.3; Surrender is a third `StanceMatrix` choice (offered at war and weaker, weight a logistic of the loss share, its weight is its probability) and an order (`OrderTypeSurrender`, `GameAI.ApplySurrender`) that sets both stances to Peace and a 30-turn truce lock (`DiplomacyState.TruceUntil`; `IsAtWar` false, Declare War refused). Logged as `Combat`, `ColonyShipsLost`, `LossDrop`, `Surrender` and `FleetHealth`. See CLAUDE.md "Ship combat" and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-ship-combat-design.md`. Tuning (6 `test2.json` runs against 6, 6 `4p.json` against 3): planets and arrivals stay in the earlier ranges, 174-560 warships destroyed per run (about half of those built), fleet health at T300 93-100%, 0-2 colony ships lost per run, no declaration inside any of 26 truces, fleet-cap violations 0; wars and flips rise on `test2.json` (17-26 against 12-17 wars) and blockade events and hostility saturation fall on `4p.json`. *(moved 2026-10-05)*
  - Engaged-force loss share and recalibrated surrender terms done (follow-up to (2)): the loss share that drives the hostility loss drop and the Surrender weight is now the strength drop the rival took off me over the 10-turn window (every fight round's fall in my strength, damage included and destroyed ships in full, split among attackers by pool share: `CombatLoss.StrengthDrop`) divided by my strength engaged against it at the fight planets (`CombatLoss.Engaged`, the victim's start-of-turn strength split the same way); `CombatSystem` now appends a `WarshipsLost` result on every fight turn (0 lost when nothing died) so the denominator is complete, and the per-planet core is the pure `CombatSystem.Round`. The old share divided by the whole fleet and reached only 0.02-0.14, which left surrender and the loss drop dormant; a first numerator that counted only the strength of ships at the round they died read about 0.004 for a wipe-out by attrition and was replaced after the review. Recalibrated from Play logs to `significantLossFraction` 0.15, `surrenderMidpoint` 0.3, `surrenderSteepness` 0.03 (0.08 / 0.15 / 0.015 made about half of all wars end by surrender, 14-33 a run); with them about a third do, at a median share of 0.31-0.37, and the owner called that rate fine. The `Hostility` line gained an `engagedStrength` field. See CLAUDE.md "Ship combat" and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-retreat-and-engaged-loss-share-design.md`. *(moved 2026-10-06)*
  - Retreat done (follow-up to (2)): after combat each turn `GameAI.AppendFightProjections` appends a `FightProjection` result per (planet, player) in a war fight (`FightProjector`, pure: the fight played to its end with `CombatSystem.Round`, plus my own in-flight ships landing there, plus 8 samples with the rival seen at 1 +- 25% of its offense and health for imperfect intel); `RetreatPlanner` runs first in `PlayerAI.PlanShipActions`: a deterministic gate on the perceived values (mean loss at least `retreatCheckFraction` and the rival surviving in half the samples; at a planet I populate only a wipe-out in half the samples), then `RetreatMatrix` (stance-matrix pattern, `IndependentRows`) weighs Stay against each Retreat destination by a roulette (`1 / (1 + e^(-(loss - 0.5) / 0.1))` times a cost-weighted share, `(cheapest / cost)^2`, of every valid destination in the best tier). Destination tiers: a planet I populate; a known empty planet or one populated only by players I am not at war with; a war rival's planet whose remembered blockade is under my group's offense / (1 + `blockadeBreakMargin`); tiers 1 and 2 hold no war-rival warship and are not blockaded by a war rival, and the route avoids war-rival blockades (`BlockadeView.OnlyFrom`). A retreat is the ordinary ship-transport order trio (no new order type); a retreating planet leaves the garrison planner, is off the assault target list and is not refilled for `retreatCooldownTurns` (`ShipTransportPlanner.Retreating/RefillBlocked`, `AssaultPlanner.ExcludedTargets`). Logged as `Retreat`, `RetreatStay`, `RetreatHeld` and `RetreatArrive`. Also done in the same work: `PlanetUpdateResult` renamed `UpdateResult`. Tuning (6 + 6 + 3 + 3 + 1 + 1 `test2.json` and `4p.json` runs): ships destroyed per run fell from 176-330 (`test2.json`) and 240-508 (`4p.json`) to about 70-230 and 60-150 with planets at T375, colonize arrivals and fleet-cap discipline in the earlier ranges, no declaration inside a surrender truce and no retreat-cooldown violation; the destination share to a planet's most common destination fell from 69-90% to 45%. See CLAUDE.md "Ship combat" (Retreat) and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-06-retreat-and-engaged-loss-share-design.md`. *(moved 2026-10-06)*
  - (3) Planetary invasion done: a planet changes hands by dominance and conversion, not troops (`ConversionSystem`, pure, run right after combat, off in legacy mode). A player dominates a planet when its warships with effective offense are docked there and no warship of a player at war with it is (a truce is not war; two non-war dominators: more offense, then the lower id). A session starts only against a holder it is at war with; each turn progress grows by `1 / max(1, conversionTurnsBase x (1 - my share of the inhabitants))` (`conversionTurnsBase` 3) and each time it reaches 1 one inhabitant flips (at-war holders first, largest then lowest id, then the non-war tail) until the planet is clean, dominance is lost (a rival warship arrives, 52 to 56% of sessions) or the war ends; an ownership change clears the production item and queue (no refund), improvements, stocks and morale pass to the new owner; hostility `hostilityPerConversion` 3 toward the converter for each inhabitant converted from a player it is NOT at war with, recorded on `DiplomacyState` and charged by the victim's `UpdateDiplomacy`. State on `Planet` (`ConversionBy`, `ConversionProgress`, `ConversionWarMask`) is saved (`conversionBy` as id + 1, so older saves load with none). The AI side: `AssaultPlanner.ConversionHolds` and `ConversionKeep` keep a converting fleet where it is but release ships beyond `max(1, ceil(conversionHoldKeepFraction (0.5) x nearby rival offense / offense per ship))` to garrison and blockade calls (the assault target and contested holds keep every ship); `IsValidColonizationTarget` accepts a dominated planet where a conversion can run and I hold an inhabitant, with its choice cost divided by `1 + conversionColonizeWeight (0.5) x (1 - my share)`; a `ConversionHoldSpare` audit reruns the planners without any keep to log the full demand. Tuning-log lines `ConversionStart`, `Convert`, `ConversionEnd`, `OwnerChanged`, `PlayerOutOfPlanets`, `ConversionHoldSpare` (with `keep` and `released`), `ConversionColonize` and a trailing `conversionTerm` on `Stance` and `Hostility`; self-checks `ConversionSelfCheck` and a conversion case in `SimultaneitySelfCheck`. Tuned on `test2.json` and `4p.json` logs: `conversionTurnsBase` 6 to 3 (real conquests finishing 5% to 16 to 20%, about 12 conquests a run against 3, nothing else moved), keep fraction 0.25 tried and reverted (4p blockade events and shipment cuts doubled), 12 tried (conquest nearly stopped). A final-review fix narrowed colonization of a dominated planet to planets where a conversion can run; a conquered capitol (the owned Prime) crashed the research notifications until `GameAIMap.GetPlayerCapitolName` returned empty for a player without one.
