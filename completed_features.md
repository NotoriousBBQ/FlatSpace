  - (1) Simplified diplomacy simulation done: every ordered pair of players holds a Peace or War stance (`DiplomacyState` on `GameAIMap`), driven by a per-pair hostility score that each turn decays 5% and gains the rival's blockade cuts on my orders (x5), its warships docked on my planets or beside them (x0.5, not its garrison on a planet it populates), and 1 x log2(my fleet strength / its visible fleet strength) clamped to +-2 (`FleetStrength` = sum of Offense x (Health + Defense)). `StanceMatrix` decides War or Peace per rival (a `ScoreMatrix` with the new `IndependentRows` flag; a logistic of the score with midpoint 30 and steepness 4, the held stance x3, a 10-turn hold); war needs only one side and the other side is forced into it once it has contact with the declarer. War puts a Consolidate player into Amass and peace with every rival returns it to Consolidate; Amass runs the Expand routine with its own research and industry tables (Warship 4.0, ColonyShip 1.0 on industry) and inherits every "Consolidate only" rule through `PlayerAI.IsConsolidateLike`. The assault attacks only players at war (the force is sized from their fleets only, `WantedWarships` adds the assault force only against a war rival, and `ContestedHolds` releases a standoff on a peaceful rival's planet unless it is blockaded against me); a war without contact decays and can end. Stances persist in saves (`PlayerSave.stances`); `diplomacyEnabled` false (and any self-check map) is the legacy mode with every rival an enemy. Logged as `Stance`, `WarForced` and `Hostility`. See CLAUDE.md "Diplomacy" and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-04-diplomacy-design.md`.   - (2) Ship to ship combat done: docked warships of players at war that share a planet fight once a turn (`CombatSystem.Resolve`, pure, run after arrivals and before the planet update, off in legacy mode): a start-of-turn snapshot, each side's effective offense split among its war rivals by hostility toward each (floor 1), one pool per victim, focus fire on the lowest-health-fraction ship scaled by `K / (K + Defense)` (`combatDamageK` 20), applied simultaneously, losses attributed per attacker (`PlanetUpdateResultTypeWarshipsLost`). Damage is persistent (`Ship.Damage`, stored so older saves load at full health), travels with a fleet in flight and in saves (`ShipFleetPayload.Damage`, `ShipSave.damage`), and a damaged ship deals proportionally less (`WarshipStats.EffectiveOffense`, no floor), which every offense reader uses (strength, blockade, assault, in-flight offense, fleet row); warships heal 10% a turn at a planet their owner populates with no at-war warship present, and a player's colony ships die when it has no warship at a planet and an at-war rival has one (everywhere). Hostility gains 1 per ship lost and loses 4 a turn while the share of fleet strength lost over 10 turns is at least 0.3; Surrender is a third `StanceMatrix` choice (offered at war and weaker, weight a logistic of the loss share, its weight is its probability) and an order (`OrderTypeSurrender`, `GameAI.ApplySurrender`) that sets both stances to Peace and a 30-turn truce lock (`DiplomacyState.TruceUntil`; `IsAtWar` false, Declare War refused). Logged as `Combat`, `ColonyShipsLost`, `LossDrop`, `Surrender` and `FleetHealth`. See CLAUDE.md "Ship combat" and `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-10-05-ship-combat-design.md`.   - (3) Planetary invasion done: a planet changes hands by dominance and conversion, not troops (`ConversionSystem`, pure, run right after combat, off in legacy mode).
